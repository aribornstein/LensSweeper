Sequence: approach → contact pulse → corner confirmation → cyan scan → amber clue emergence → quiet settled clue.

This GIF mechanically crops six generated storyboard panels and holds them for review. It is a slowed keyframe animatic, not smooth generated video or runtime footage. Minor foot and background differences are image-generation artifacts.

Suggested runtime timing after tile entry:
- 0–80ms: brighten the nearest corners.
- 80–160ms: pulse all four corners.
- 120–320ms: sweep a thin cyan scan across the cell.
- 240–520ms: raise and fade in the player-facing spatial clue.
- 520–650ms: fade transient effects and settle.

Keep the board world anchored and the real pavement visible throughout. The shoe is a concept cue for entry; this proposal does not require foot tracking or foot rendering. Trigger from existing player tile-entry detection. Empty safe tiles use the same confirmation and scan without a number.
